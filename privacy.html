Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

Overview

Ghost Beat is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data Collection

Ghost Beat does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-up required
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising
  • No data sent to any server
  • No cookies or web tracking

Data Storage

All app data — including your settings, presets, practice routines, and session history — is stored locally on your device using standard iOS/macOS storage (UserDefaults). This data is not accessible to us or any third party.

In-App Purchases

Ghost Beat offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (Ghost Beat Pro). Purchases are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store any payment information. Purchase records are managed by your Apple ID.

Notifications

Ghost Beat may request permission to send local notifications for practice streak reminders and weekly summaries. These notifications are generated entirely on your device — no data is sent externally. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Microphone

Ghost Beat does not access your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or any other device sensors.

Children's Privacy

Ghost Beat does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Third-Party Services

Ghost Beat does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
