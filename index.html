Built for Serious Practice

Ghost Beat isn't just a metronome — it's a timing trainer. Every feature is designed to help you develop rock-solid internal pulse.

👻

Random Mute

Ghost Beat randomly silences beats so you have to keep time internally. When the click disappears, your real timing is revealed.

🥁

Beat Grid

Visual grid of every beat in the bar. Toggle individual beats on or off, set accents, and customize your click pattern completely.

🎯

Accent Control

Tap any beat in the grid to accent it. Beat 1 is accented by default. Build any accent pattern you want — triplets, clave, anything.

📉

Progressive Difficulty

Beats gradually fade out over time, forcing you to internalize the pulse before they disappear completely. The ultimate timing test.

📋

Practice Routines

Chain presets into timed practice sessions. Set a BPM progression, work through your routine, and track how far you've come.

📊

Tap Accuracy

Tap along and see exactly how close you are to the beat. Stats panel shows your average deviation and consistency over time.

🎛️

Jog Wheel BPM

Scroll the jog wheel to nudge BPM up or down in real time — no typing required. Fast, tactile, like a real piece of hardware.

🔊

Background Audio

Ghost Beat keeps playing while you use other apps. Switch to your DAW, sheet music, or YouTube — the click keeps running.

How It Works

1

Set your tempo

Dial in BPM with the jog wheel or type it directly. Set your time signature and subdivisions for the feel you want.

2

Customize the grid

Turn beats on or off. Tap to accent. Enable random mute to introduce silence, or turn on progressive difficulty to fade the click out.

3

Practice and listen

Play along. When beats drop out, keep going. Your job is to stay exactly where the click would be — even when it's not there.

4

Track your accuracy

Use the tap accuracy panel to see how locked in you really are. Numbers don't lie — and improvement shows up fast.
