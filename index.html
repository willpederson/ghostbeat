Survival Training for Your Internal Clock

Every feature is designed around one goal: making you keep time without relying on the click.

👻

Ghost Bars

Set how many bars play and how many go silent. 2 on, 2 off. 1 on, 3 off. When the click vanishes, your real timing shows up.

🎲

Random Mute

Individual beats disappear unpredictably. You never know which one is next. Pure internal time training.

🎯

Tap Accuracy

Tap along with the beat and get graded S through D. Calibrates to your touch latency, then measures real timing deviation.

📋

Practice Routines

Chain exercises into timed sessions. Set BPM, on/off bars, and time signature per step. Hit play and the routine runs itself.

🏆

Daily Challenges

A new timing challenge every day — survive ghost bars, hit accuracy targets, or hold tempo for a set time. Stay sharp.

📊

Practice Stats

Streaks, session history, timing grades, and weekly charts. See your progress measured in timing-specific terms, not just minutes.

📉

How It Works

1

Set your tempo and ghost bars

Dial in BPM with the jog wheel. Set ON bars (click plays) and OFF bars (silence). Start simple: 3 on, 1 off.

2

Play through the silence

When the click drops out, keep going. Stay exactly where the beat would be. When it comes back, see if you drifted.

3

Measure your timing

Turn on Tap Accuracy and tap along. Get graded on every tap — see your deviation in milliseconds and watch your grade climb.

4

Push harder

Increase off bars, enable random mute, try daily challenges. Build the kind of timing that doesn't need a safety net.
